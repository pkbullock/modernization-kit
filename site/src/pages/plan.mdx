## Guidance for the PLAN stage of the workflow

After running an assessment and collecting the scope of the page transformation, agree an initial page inventory with site owners, including candidates to retain,
modernise, or retire.

* Agree scope, delivery roles, and a representative pilot with site owners.
* Plan target layouts and mapping requirements for the selected pages.
* Define review criteria, rollout communications, and a recovery approach.

## Change Management

Page modernisation is not only a technical change. People using teh existing site and site owners will need to understand what is changing, when it is changing, and what support is available to them. A simple change management plan will help coordinate responsibilities, communications, content reviews, and the transition to the new experience.

Set up clear routes for users to raise issues and queries during the transition. This could include dedicated queues in a third-party service such as ServiceNow for support requests, alongside a community channel where people can ask questions, share solutions, and learn from others.
